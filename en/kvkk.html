1. Data Controller

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”), the data controller for personal data processed through the Trabzon Keşfet mobile app (the “App”) is Taha Sarı (“Trabzon Keşfet”), whose contact details are given below.

Taha Sarı (Trabzon Keşfet)

Address: İnönü Mahallesi, Yavuz Selim Bulvarı No: 284/A, Ortahisar, Trabzon, Türkiye

Email: trabzonkesfet@gmail.com

Phone: +90 544 408 91 00

This notice is provided under Article 10 of the KVKK and the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Fulfilling the Obligation to Inform. Trabzon Keşfet is an independent city guide; it is not an app of any municipality, public authority or official body.

2. In Short

  • The App requires no registration or account; you do not need to provide any personal information to use it.
  • Your location, contacts and microphone are not accessed; no advertising, analytics or user-tracking software is used.
  • Information such as your name, email or phone number is processed only when you fill in and submit a form yourself.
  • Coupon codes you obtain are not linked to your identity.

3. Everyone Using the App

  • Data processed: Transaction security data: technical connection data such as IP address, connection time, app version and operating system type. Customer transaction data: the coupon code you obtain, the related business and the time the coupon was created/redeemed (without being linked to your identity).
  • Purpose: Providing the App content (businesses, bus times and fares, charging stations, images, weather); creating coupons and enabling their redemption at businesses; keeping the service secure and preventing misuse.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) (necessary for the performance of the agreement formed by the Terms of Use) and Art. 5(2)(f) (our legitimate interest in providing the service securely and without interruption).
  • Method of collection: Automatically and electronically when the App connects to servers.

Your language and theme preferences, the weather location you choose, the last weather forecast (up to 24 hours), bus timetables (up to 14 days) and the coupon code you obtain (up to 24 hours) are stored only on your device and are not sent to Trabzon Keşfet. The Keşfet Assistant runs on your device; your questions are not sent to any server.

4. Contact and Feedback Form

  • Data processed: Identity: full name. Contact: email address. Customer transaction: subject, message or suggestion, and the content the report relates to (e.g. a place name). For technical error reports, unless you switch it off, the app version and platform.
  • Purpose: Receiving and evaluating your request, suggestion or error report and replying to you; correcting and improving content; resolving requests made under the KVKK.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(f) (our legitimate interest in replying to your requests); for KVKK requests, Art. 5(2)(ç) (compliance with the controller’s legal obligation).
  • Method of collection: Electronically, when you fill in and submit the form.
  • Retention period: Up to 2 years from submission; deleted automatically afterwards.

5. Business Application

  • Data processed: Identity: name of the authorised person. Contact: phone, email, business address and district. Other: business name, category, opening hours and description.
  • Purpose: Evaluating the application, contacting the business, publishing approved businesses in the App and running the coupon/campaign partnership.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) (directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract) and Art. 5(2)(f).
  • Method of collection: Electronically, when you fill in and submit the application form.
  • Retention period: The application record: up to 2 years from the application date. The public information of a published business (name, address, phone, description, images) is shown for as long as the business is listed in the App.

6. Partner Businesses (Business Mode)

  • Data processed: Transaction security: an irreversible hash of the PIN given to the business, the number of failed attempts, a hash of the session key and session start/end times. Customer transaction: redeemed coupon codes and redemption times.
  • Purpose: Ensuring that only authorised staff can redeem coupons; preventing PIN guessing and coupon misuse; tracking coupon quotas.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and Art. 5(2)(f).
  • Retention period: The session key is kept in the device’s encrypted storage for up to 12 hours; expired session records on the server are deleted 1 day after expiry. The PIN hash is kept until the PIN is changed or the partnership ends.

When a QR code is scanned, the camera image is processed instantly on the device only; it is not recorded or sent to any server.

7. Administrators

  • Data processed: Identity/contact: administrator email address. Transaction security: an irreversible hash of the password, session keys, sign-in time and IP address.
  • Purpose: Ensuring that only authorised persons can access the admin panel, and securing that access.
  • Legal basis: KVKK Art. 5(2)(c) and Art. 5(2)(f).
  • Retention period: For as long as the person acts as an administrator. The session is kept in the device’s encrypted storage until sign-out.

8. Recipients of Personal Data

Your personal data may be transferred to the parties below only for the purposes above and only to the extent necessary. Your personal data is not sold, rented or shared for advertising.

  • Supabase Inc. (processor): Database, file storage and administrator authentication. Data is hosted in the European Union, in the Frankfurt (Germany) data centre.
  • Google LLC: Only on Android devices, the Google ML Kit library used for scanning QR codes in Business Mode may send diagnostic information to Google under Google’s terms, such as device model, operating system and app version, performance metrics, error codes and a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify a person or device. The camera image is not sent.
  • Content providers: Open-Meteo for weather, Trabzon Metropolitan Municipality (ulasim.trabzon.bel.tr) for bus timetables, and Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash for place and business images. While this content loads, your device connects directly to the relevant server; your IP address, the app version and the content of the request (e.g. the coordinates of the district you chose or a line number) are processed by these providers under their own policies. Your location is not sent.
  • Competent public authorities: Authorised authorities, institutions and courts, where required by law and upon a lawful request.

When you tap a map, WhatsApp, phone or email link, the relevant app opens on your device; from then on that service’s own privacy terms apply.

9. International Transfers

Supabase’s data centre is in Germany; the infrastructure of Google, Wikimedia and Unsplash may be located outside Türkiye, including in the USA. The personal data described above is therefore transferred abroad.

Transfers abroad are made in accordance with Article 9 of the KVKK and the Regulation on the Procedures and Principles for the Transfer of Personal Data Abroad; where there is no adequacy decision of the Personal Data Protection Board, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contracts signed with processors and notified to the Board.

10. Retention and Deletion

Personal data is kept for as long as required by the purpose of processing and by applicable law; it is then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

  • Contact and feedback records: 2 years from submission.
  • Business applications: 2 years from the application date.
  • Coupon records: 2 years from creation (not linked to your identity).
  • Business Mode sessions: 1 day after the session ends.
  • Infrastructure logs: Technical request logs of hosting providers containing IP addresses, for the short periods set by the provider and at most 90 days.

Expired records are deleted automatically every day. Records that must be kept because of a dispute or a legal obligation may be kept for as long as that reason requires.

11. Your Rights

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • request information about the processing;
  • learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used accordingly;
  • know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom your personal data is transferred;
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data;
  • request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK;
  • request that correction, deletion or destruction be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred;
  • object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems;
  • claim compensation for damage arising from unlawful processing.

12. How to Apply

You may submit requests regarding your rights, in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, by one of the following means:

  • a signed letter delivered in person or through a notary to the address above;
  • from your registered electronic mail (KEP) address, or signed with a secure electronic or mobile signature, to trabzonkesfet@gmail.com;
  • from an email address you have previously given us and that is registered in our system (e.g. the address you used in a form) to trabzonkesfet@gmail.com;
  • through Menu → General Contact in the App, choosing the “Personal data request (KVKK)” topic.

Your request must include your full name, Turkish ID number (for foreign nationals, nationality and passport or ID number), your residential or business address for notifications, your email address and phone number if any, and the subject of your request. We may ask for additional information to verify your identity.

Your request will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest. If the process requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Board.

If your request is rejected, you find the answer insufficient or no answer is given in time, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of the answer and in any case within 60 days of your application.

13. Other Matters

The “I have read the KVKK privacy notice.” box in the forms only confirms that you have read this notice; it is not a declaration of explicit consent. The App does not rely on your explicit consent for the processing described here.

This notice is updated when processing activities or the law change. The current version is published in the App and at https://mustafaatunc.github.io/trabzon-kesfet-yasal/en/kvkk.html.
