1. General

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Trabzon Keşfet mobile app (the “App”) processes, why and how, with whom it is shared and what your rights are. The App is an independent city guide that brings together places to visit, transport information, local businesses and discount coupons in Trabzon. It is not an official app of any municipality or public authority.

The App is published by Taha Sarı (“Trabzon Keşfet”, “we”), who is the data controller for your personal data. Contact details are given in the last section.

The purposes and legal bases of processing and your rights under the Turkish data protection law (KVKK) are explained in detail in the Privacy Notice (KVKK). Privacy Notice (KVKK) →

2. Summary

  • No account or registration: you do not need to provide personal information to use the App.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking: the App contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or user-tracking software, and no profile is created about you.
  • No location: location permission is never requested; the district or point you choose is used for weather and directions.
  • Only what you send: information such as your name, email or phone number is collected only when you fill in and submit a form.
  • No selling: your information is not sold, rented or shared for advertising.

3. Information We Collect

  • Forms: Contact and feedback form: full name, email, subject and message. Business application: business name, category, name of the authorised person, phone, email, district, address, opening hours and description. Technical error reports include the app version and platform (e.g. Android) unless you switch off “Include app information”.
  • Coupons: When you obtain a coupon from a business, the code, business, coupon status and creation/redemption time are recorded. This record is not linked to your name, device or any account.
  • Technical connection data: When the App connects to servers, the IP address, request time, app version and operating system type are transmitted. This is used to deliver content and for security.
  • Business Mode: For partner businesses, a hash of the PIN, a hash of the session key and redeemed coupon records.
  • Admin panel: Only for authorised administrators: email address and session data, and business images uploaded by the administrator.

4. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used only in Business Mode, when business staff want to scan a customer’s coupon QR code and grant permission. The image is processed instantly on the device; it is not recorded or sent to any server. Without the permission the coupon code can be typed in.
  • Photos: Only in the admin panel, when an administrator chooses a business image, the system photo picker opens and only the selected image is uploaded. General access to the photo library is not requested.
  • Internet: To update content and submit forms.

The App does not access location, contacts, microphone, notifications, calendar or health data.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Preferences: Language, theme (light/dark) and the weather location you choose.
  • Offline use: The last weather forecast for up to 24 hours and bus timetables for up to 14 days.
  • Coupon code: Up to 24 hours, so that you can show it again at the till; deleted when it expires or the coupon is redeemed.
  • Sessions: The Business Mode session for up to 12 hours and the administrator session until sign-out; kept in the operating system’s encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and never moved to another device.

This information is not sent to Trabzon Keşfet. You can delete all of it by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in your device settings. The App does not use cookies. The Keşfet Assistant runs on your device; your questions are not sent to any server.

6. How We Use Information

  • to reply to your requests, suggestions and error reports and to correct content;
  • to evaluate business applications and publish suitable businesses;
  • to create coupons and enable their redemption at businesses;
  • to deliver the App content;
  • to keep the service secure and prevent misuse and fraud;
  • to comply with legal obligations and resolve KVKK requests.

Your information is not used for advertising, marketing, profiling or automated decision-making. We do not send you commercial electronic messages.

7. Who We Share Information With

  • Supabase (database and storage): Data is hosted in the EU, in the Frankfurt (Germany) data centre.
  • Google ML Kit (Android only, Business Mode): QR codes are read on the device; ML Kit may send Google diagnostic information such as device model, version information, performance metrics and a per-installation identifier. The image is not sent.
  • Content providers: Open-Meteo (weather), Trabzon Metropolitan Municipality (bus times), Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash (images). Your device connects directly to these servers; your IP address and request data are processed by these providers. Your location is not sent.
  • Authorities: Only where required by law and upon a lawful request.

Some of these providers are outside Türkiye; information on international transfers is in the Privacy Notice (KVKK). The App is distributed through Google Play and the App Store; downloads and updates are subject to those stores’ own privacy policies.

8. Retention Periods

  • Contact and feedback records: 2 years
  • Business applications: 2 years
  • Coupon records (anonymous): 2 years
  • Business Mode session records: 1 day after the session ends
  • Infrastructure logs: at most 90 days

Expired records are deleted automatically every day.

9. Security

  • All connections are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS); unencrypted connections are not allowed.
  • Row-level access rules are enforced in the database; only authorised administrators can see form records and messages.
  • Business PINs and session keys are stored on the server as irreversible hashes; PIN attempts are limited.
  • Session keys on the device are kept in the operating system’s encrypted storage.
  • On Android, screenshots and screen recording are blocked while the admin panel or Business Mode is open.
  • Only keys that are safe to ship in a client app are embedded in the App; keys with administrative privileges are never included.

No method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If a security breach affects your personal data, the data subjects and the Personal Data Protection Board will be notified in accordance with Article 12 of the KVKK.

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are under 18, please use the forms with your parent’s or guardian’s knowledge. If you become aware that a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Your Rights and Deleting Your Data

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to access your data, request its correction or deletion, and object.

To have a form or application you submitted deleted, simply email trabzonkesfet@gmail.com from the address you used in the form, or choose the “Personal data request (KVKK)” topic in Menu → General Contact in the App. Once your identity is verified, the record is deleted within 30 days at the latest and you are informed. As the App has no user accounts, no separate account deletion is needed.

The full application procedure and all your rights are set out in the Privacy Notice (KVKK). Privacy Notice (KVKK) →

12. Third-Party Links

Map, WhatsApp, phone, email and web links open in the relevant app on your device. Trabzon Keşfet is not responsible for the privacy practices of these services; their own policies apply.

13. Changes

This policy may be updated when the App’s processing activities or the law change. Significant changes are announced in the App; the current version and the last updated date are shown on this page.
