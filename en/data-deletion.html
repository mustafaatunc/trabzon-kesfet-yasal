Deleting Your Data

The Trabzon Keşfet app has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. On our servers, only the forms you submit (contact, feedback, business application) contain personal data.

Deleting a form you submitted

  • Email trabzonkesfet@gmail.com from the address you used in the form with the subject “Data deletion request”, or
  • in the app, choose the “Personal data request (KVKK)” topic in Menu → General Contact.

Please state the date and the name under which you sent the form. Once your identity is verified, the record is permanently deleted within 30 days at the latest and you are informed.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the name, email, phone, address and message in the form.
  • Deleted automatically: all form records after 2 years, expired Business Mode sessions after 1 day; infrastructure logs are kept for at most 90 days.
  • Not personal data: coupon records are not linked to your identity; they are deleted after 2 years.
  • May be kept: records that must be kept because of a dispute or a legal obligation, for as long as that reason requires.

Data on your device

Language, theme, weather location, offline data and coupon codes are kept only on your device. You can delete them immediately by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.
